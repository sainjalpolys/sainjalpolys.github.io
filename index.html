        Project 01Ongoing

        Cloud-native
        logistics platform

        Role — Solution Architect

        1. Container moves planned and priced across many legs, partners and teams — in one platform that can keep up.

        2. Microservices on Google Cloud, event-driven by default, secured at the edge with single sign-on and runtime role-based access.

        3. Dynamic access control, cost and route optimisation for multi-leg moves, a transactional outbox to Pub/Sub, and search on Elasticsearch.

        4. Containerised services on Cloud Run that scale on demand, with CI/CD on every change, managed secrets and central logging.

        5. A platform built to grow with the business — new partners, new rules and new regions, without rewrites.
